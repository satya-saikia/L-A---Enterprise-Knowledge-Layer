# Life & Annuity (L&A) Insurance Organization — Logical Data Model

This document provides the complete **Logical Data Model** derived from `Output/L&A Data Model - Conceptual.MD` across all **16 Enterprise Domains**, covering enterprise general account vintage bucketing, separate account variable accumulation/annuity dual-unit accounting, institutional retirement plan hierarchies, and lifetime income payout structures.

All entity names and attribute names follow strict business-friendly naming standards, every definition exceeds 50 characters with rich insurance and retirement business context, and sample values are included for domain attributes.

---

## 1. Logical Entity Catalog (Tabular Output)

| Domain Name | Entity Name | Entity Definitions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product** | **Product Line** | Product Line entity defines the high-level executive and regulatory categorization of Life and Annuity offerings marketed by the organization, grouping individual products by tax qualification structure, institutional versus retail target market, and statutory line of business reporting. |
| **Product** | **Product** | Product entity holds the master catalog definition of each marketable Life Insurance or Annuity contract design (such as Participating General Account Retirement Annuity, Separate Account Variable Annuity, Fixed Indexed Annuity, or Universal Life), capturing issue age boundaries, contribution limits, account structure, and surrender charge schedules. |
| **Product** | **Product State Filing & Availability** | Product State Filing & Availability entity tracks the regulatory submission, Interstate Insurance Product Regulation Commission (IIPRC) or state Department of Insurance approval status, policy form numbers, and channel selling eligibility for each product across every US state jurisdiction. |
| **Product** | **Product Crediting & Liquidity Rule** | Product Crediting & Liquidity Rule entity defines the contractual interest rate floors, liquidity restrictions, and mandatory multi-year installment withdrawal rules (such as 10-year Transfer Payout Annuity [TPA] or 84-month Retirement Choice [RC] rules) governing general account and fixed products. |
| **Policy** | **Policy** | Policy entity holds the core contract and certificate master information for all Life Insurance policies and Annuity contracts ever issued or administered by the carrier, linking the underlying product, institutional retirement plan, administration system,company code, valuation balances, and lifecycle status. |
| **Policy** | **Policy Coverage Segment** | Policy Coverage Segment entity represents an individual benefit layer or coverage tranche within a Policy (such as base life coverage, supplemental term layer, or paid-up additions), storing the specific face amount, mortality risk classification, underwriting rating, and premium payment mode. |
| **Policy** | **Policy Vintage Bucket Balance** | Policy Vintage Bucket Balance entity tracks contributions and accumulated general account values grouped by the specific calendar window ("vintage") in which premiums were received, enabling vintage-specific guaranteed interest rates, board-declared excess dividends, and liquidity rules. |
| **Policy** | **Policy Loan** | Policy Loan entity records collateralized loans taken by a policyholder or retirement plan participant against their policy cash surrender value or vested retirement plan accumulation, tracking principal balances, accrued loan interest, repayment schedules, and IRS 72(p) deemed distribution defaults. |
| **Riders** | **Rider Product Definition** | Rider Product Definition entity catalogs all optional and embedded supplemental benefit guarantees offered by the carrier—including Guaranteed Minimum Withdrawal Benefits (GMWB), Guaranteed Minimum Income Benefits (GMIB), Guaranteed Minimum Death Benefits (GMDB), and Waiver of Premium riders—along with fee formulas and roll-up rules. |
| **Riders** | **Policy Rider Coverage** | Policy Rider Coverage entity represents an active or historical instance of a supplemental Rider Product attached to a specific Policy, capturing the election effective date, rider status, current annual charge rate deducted from the contract, and waiting period completion. |
| **Riders** | **Rider Benefit Base Valuation** | Rider Benefit Base Valuation entity records periodic point-in-time valuations of a policy rider's guaranteed benefit base, tracking step-up high-water marks, compound roll-up bases, maximum annual lifetime withdrawal allowances, and pro-rata reductions caused by excess withdrawals. |
| **Fund** | **Investment Fund** | Investment Fund entity defines each underlying investment vehicle available within Separate Account variable products (such as Variable Equity, Direct Real Estate Separate Account, and target-date sub-accounts), Indexed Annuity strategies, and General Account fixed options, storing asset class, expense ratios, and Assumed Investment Return (AIR) rates. |
| **Fund** | **Fund Unit Price** | Fund Unit Price entity stores the daily or periodic net asset valuation for each Investment Fund, capturing both the Accumulation Unit Value (AUV) used during the savings phase and the Annuity Unit Value used to adjust variable lifetime income payments during the payout phase. |
| **Fund** | **Policy Fund Allocation Election** | Policy Fund Allocation Election entity captures the policyholder's or participant's standing instructions for how future premium contributions, automatic portfolio rebalancing, and dollar-cost averaging transfers are split across eligible Investment Funds. |
| **Fund** | **Policy Valuation Account** | Policy Valuation Account entity holds the current sub-account position and unit balance for a specific Policy within a specific Investment Fund, distinguishing between accumulation-phase units, payout-phase annuity units, total market value, and surrender-free value. |
| **Transactions** | **Financial Transaction** | Financial Transaction entity serves as the immutable financial activity register for a Policy, recording every premium contribution, employer match, rollover, fund-to-fund transfer, systematic withdrawal, surrender, fee deduction, and annuity payout along with gross, tax, penalty, and net amounts. |
| **Transactions** | **Transaction Fund Allocation Detail** | Transaction Fund Allocation Detail entity breaks down a parent Financial Transaction into individual fund-level and vintage-level debits or credits, recording the exact dollar amount, unit price applied, number of accumulation or annuity units bought or sold, and vintage bucket assigned. |
| **Transactions** | **Non-Financial Service Transaction** | Non-Financial Service Transaction entity logs administrative and contractual maintenance endorsements performed on a Policy that do not immediately move money—such as beneficiary changes, ownership transfers, address updates, and future allocation changes—for audit and customer service traceability. |
| **Reinsurance** | **Reinsurer Counterparty** | Reinsurer Counterparty entity stores master legal, regulatory, NAIC identification, and creditworthiness information for external third-party and affiliated captive reinsurance companies that assume mortality, longevity, or market risk from the carrier. |
| **Reinsurance** | **Reinsurance Treaty** | Reinsurance Treaty entity defines the formal contractual agreement between the insurance carrier and a Reinsurer Counterparty (such as Yearly Renewable Term, Coinsurance, Modified Coinsurance, or Longevity Swap treaties), specifying retention limits, cession percentages, and experience refund rules. |
| **Reinsurance** | **Reinsured Policy Cession** | Reinsured Policy Cession entity tracks the policy-level risk ceded under a Reinsurance Treaty for a specific Policy and valuation period, recording direct face amount, retained risk, ceded Net Amount at Risk (NAR), ceded statutory reserves, and reinsurance premiums due. |
| **Reinsurance** | **Reinsurance Claim Recovery** | Reinsurance Claim Recovery entity manages the billing, tracking, and cash collection of reinsured benefit amounts recoverable from a Reinsurer Counterparty when a covered Policy experiences a death, disability, or annuity payout claim. |
| **Finance** | **General Ledger Account** | General Ledger Account entity defines the enterprise insurance Chart of Accounts used to classify assets, liabilities, policyholder surplus, revenues, and expenses across US GAAP, Statutory (NAIC Blue Book), and Tax accounting bases for both the General Account and Separate Accounts. |
| **Finance** | **Accounting Period** | Accounting Period entity controls the financial reporting calendar (fiscal months, quarters, and years) and ledger close lifecycle across GAAP and Statutory books, ensuring sub-ledger entries are posted to open and auditable valuation periods. |
| **Finance** | **Sub-Ledger Journal Entry** | Sub-Ledger Journal Entry entity captures the accounting header generated by policy administration transactions, actuarial reserve movements, reinsurance settlements, or investment income accruals prior to summarization and posting into the enterprise General Ledger. |
| **Finance** | **Journal Entry Line** | Journal Entry Line entity holds the individual balanced debit and credit monetary lines belonging to a Sub-Ledger Journal Entry, linking each line to a specific General Ledger Account, Statutory Annual Statement line reference, and GAAP LDTI valuation cohort. |
| **Finance** | **General Account Crediting Rate Declaration** | General Account Crediting Rate Declaration entity records the Board-approved interest rates declared for fixed and general account products (such as general account contribution vintages), separating the contractual guaranteed floor rate from additional declared dividend or excess interest rates. |
| **Experience Studies** | **Experience Study** | Experience Study entity catalogs formal historical actuarial investigations into policyholder biometric and behavioral events (mortality, annuitant longevity, voluntary lapse, annuitization election, and GMWB rider utilization) over a defined observation window against industry benchmark tables. |
| **Experience Studies** | **Experience Exposure & Decrement Cohort** | Experience Exposure & Decrement Cohort entity stores the aggregated exposure counts, face/account values exposed, actual decrements (deaths, surrenders, annuitizations), expected decrements, and Actual-to-Expected (A/E) ratios segmented by age, duration, gender, and rider in-the-moneyness. |
| **Actuarial** | **Actuarial Assumption Set** | Actuarial Assumption Set entity holds version-controlled, governance-approved mortality tables, longevity improvement scales, dynamic lapse formulas, expense assumptions, and discount rate curves used to drive GAAP LDTI, Statutory Principles-Based Reserving (PBR), and pricing runs. |
| **Actuarial** | **LDTI Valuation Cohort** | LDTI Valuation Cohort entity represents the US GAAP Long-Duration Targeted Improvements (ASC 944) aggregation unit grouping policies by product and issue year cohort to track locked-in discount rates, current market discount rates, Deferred Acquisition Costs (DAC), and Market Risk Benefits (MRB). |
| **Actuarial** | **Actuarial Valuation Reserve** | Actuarial Valuation Reserve entity stores the calculated policy-level or model-point-level liability reserves across Statutory (VM-20, VM-21, VM-22, Additional Actuarial Reserves), US GAAP LDTI, and Tax bases, alongside Net Amount at Risk and Risk-Based Capital (RBC) requirements. |
| **Actuarial** | **Actuarial Cash Flow Projection** | Actuarial Cash Flow Projection entity captures period-by-period deterministic and stochastic projected inflows (premiums, investment income) and outflows (death benefits, surrenders, annuity income payments, expenses) across economic scenarios for Asset-Liability Management (ALM) and Cash Flow Testing. |
| **Agent & Agency** | **Agency** | Agency entity represents an internal wealth management division, external Broker-Dealer partner (such as LPL Financial), Independent Marketing Organization (IMO), or bank distribution firm authorized under a selling agreement to distribute and service the carrier's Life and Annuity products. |
| **Agent & Agency** | **Producer / Advisor** | Producer / Advisor entity holds the master profile of an individual licensed insurance agent, wealth management advisor, or institutional relationship manager who solicits, sells, or services policies, tracking their National Producer Number (NPN) and FINRA CRD registration. |
| **Agent & Agency** | **Producer License & Carrier Appointment** | Producer License & Carrier Appointment entity tracks each Producer's state insurance department licenses, lines of authority (Life, Variable Contracts), FINRA securities series registrations, state carrier appointments, and mandatory NAIC Best Interest / product training completions. |
| **Agent & Agency** | **Policy Producer Assignment** | Policy Producer Assignment entity links one or more Producers and Agencies to a Policy, defining their role (Writing Agent, Servicing Advisor, Override Hierarchy) and their exact commission split percentage for upfront and trail compensation. |
| **Agent & Agency** | **Compensation Schedule & Commission Statement** | Compensation Schedule & Commission Statement entity records the commission calculation rules and periodic commission payout statements for Producers and Agencies, detailing first-year commissions, renewal trails, advisory fees, and surrender chargebacks. |
| **Party, Customer & Household** | **Party** | Party entity serves as the enterprise Master Data Management (MDM) golden record for every individual person (customer, plan participant, insured, annuitant, beneficiary) and legal organization (employer, trust, estate) interacting with the carrier across all policies and claims. |
| **Party, Customer & Household** | **Party Contact & Address Profile** | Party Contact & Address Profile entity stores residential, mailing, tax reporting, and digital contact information for a Party, alongside electronic delivery (e-Delivery) consent and returned undeliverable mail (RPO) tracking flags. |
| **Party, Customer & Household** | **Household** | Household entity aggregates related individual Parties and their associated policies and retirement accounts into a single family or wealth management relationship unit to support holistic financial planning, fee tiering, and household-level assets under management (AUM) reporting. |
| **Party, Customer & Household** | **Policy Party Role** | Policy Party Role entity resolves the many-to-many relationship between Parties and Policies, defining the exact legal capacity in which a Party participates in a contract (Owner, Plan Participant, Primary Insured, Annuitant, Primary/Contingent Beneficiary, Trustee, or Payor) and beneficiary share percentages. |
| **Institutional Plan & Employer Sponsor** | **Employer Sponsor Organization** | Employer Sponsor Organization entity represents a university, hospital system, school district, research institute, or corporate employer that sponsors one or more retirement or benefit plans administered by the carrier. |
| **Institutional Plan & Employer Sponsor** | **Institutional Retirement Plan** | Institutional Retirement Plan entity captures the master plan document terms for an employer-sponsored retirement plan (such as a 403(b), 401(a), 457(b), or 401(k) plan), governing eligible products, auto-enrollment rules, plan loan limits, hardship withdrawals, and Qualified Default Investment Alternatives (QDIA). |
| **Institutional Plan & Employer Sponsor** | **Plan Eligibility & Contribution Rule** | Plan Eligibility & Contribution Rule entity defines employee class eligibility waiting periods, mandatory employee salary deferral rates, and employer basic and matching contribution formulas established by the Employer Sponsor for an Institutional Retirement Plan. |
| **Institutional Plan & Employer Sponsor** | **Plan Vesting Schedule** | Plan Vesting Schedule entity specifies the years-of-service vesting rules (immediate, cliff, or graded vesting) governing when a participant acquires non-forfeitable ownership of employer matching and basic contributions inside an Institutional Retirement Plan. |
| **Underwriting & New Business** | **Quote & Policy Illustration** | Quote & Policy Illustration entity stores pre-sale hypothetical and guaranteed ledger projections presented to a prospective customer for a Life or Annuity product, modeling future cash values, death benefits, and lifetime income streams under guaranteed and non-guaranteed rates. |
| **Underwriting & New Business** | **Application** | Application entity captures a formal new business submission or institutional plan enrollment request to purchase a Life or Annuity contract, tracking requested coverage, initial contribution, 1035 exchange / rollover details, In-Good-Order (IGO) status, and e-signature completion. |
| **Underwriting & New Business** | **Underwriting Case & Risk Decision** | Underwriting Case & Risk Decision entity manages the mortality and financial risk evaluation of an Application—spanning guaranteed issue, algorithmic accelerated underwriting, or full medical underwriting—and records the final approved risk class, table rating, or decline decision. |
| **Underwriting & New Business** | **Medical & Mortality Requirement** | Medical & Mortality Requirement entity tracks individual underwriting evidence items ordered to evaluate an insured's mortality risk, including Attending Physician Statements (APS), paramedical exams, blood/fluid labs, prescription drug (Rx) histories, MIB checks, and Motor Vehicle Reports. |
| **Claims, Annuitization & Benefit Payouts** | **Annuitization Payout Schedule** | Annuitization Payout Schedule entity governs the income phase of an annuity contract after the owner elects to convert accumulated savings into guaranteed lifetime or fixed-period income (such as Single Life, Joint & Survivor, Period Certain, or Transfer Payout Annuity [TPA] installments). |
| **Claims, Annuitization & Benefit Payouts** | **Required Minimum Distribution Schedule** | Required Minimum Distribution Schedule entity calculates and tracks the mandatory annual withdrawal amount a qualified retirement contract owner must take under IRS Internal Revenue Code Section 401(a)(9) based on prior year-end fair market value and IRS life expectancy tables. |
| **Claims, Annuitization & Benefit Payouts** | **Claim & Benefit Settlement** | Claim & Benefit Settlement entity manages the end-to-end adjudication and financial calculation of contractual benefit events—including Death Benefit claims, Living Benefit rider exercises, Disability Waivers, and Maturity settlements—from initial notice of loss to final disbursement. |
| **Claims, Annuitization & Benefit Payouts** | **Beneficiary Claim Payout Election** | Beneficiary Claim Payout Election entity records each individual beneficiary's verified claim share and elected settlement method (such as lump-sum check/ACH, spousal contract continuation, or 10-year inherited IRA stretch) under an approved Claim & Benefit Settlement. |
| **Billing, Remittance & Tax Reporting** | **Institutional Payroll Remittance Batch** | Institutional Payroll Remittance Batch entity tracks bulk payroll contribution files and wire/ACH cash remittances submitted by an Employer Sponsor for an Institutional Retirement Plan, reconciling employee pre-tax, Roth, employer match, and loan repayment totals before participant allocation. |
| **Billing, Remittance & Tax Reporting** | **Billing & Tax Withholding Schedule** | Billing & Tax Withholding Schedule entity stores a policy's recurring premium billing frequency, EFT draft instructions, grace period dates, and standing Federal (W-4P/W-4R) and State income tax withholding elections for distributions. |
| **Billing, Remittance & Tax Reporting** | **Tax Reporting Statement** | Tax Reporting Statement entity stores annual regulatory tax forms issued to policyholders and filed with the IRS and state tax authorities (including Form 1099-R for distributions/annuity payments and Form 5498 for IRA contributions, rollovers, and year-end Fair Market Value). |
| **Compliance, Suitability & Regulatory Governance** | **Suitability & Best Interest Review** | Suitability & Best Interest Review entity records the supervisory compliance evaluation of an annuity or life transaction under SEC Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI), NAIC Model Regulation #275, and ERISA fiduciary standards, verifying alignment with customer risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and replacement cost-benefit rules. |
| **Compliance, Suitability & Regulatory Governance** | **Free-Look & Rescission Tracking** | Free-Look & Rescission Tracking entity monitors the statutory post-delivery window during which a policyholder may cancel a newly issued contract without surrender penalty, governing whether refund calculations follow Return of Premium or Return of Account Value rules. |
| **Compliance, Suitability & Regulatory Governance** | **Unclaimed Property & Escheatment Case** | Unclaimed Property & Escheatment Case entity tracks policies flagged via Social Security Death Master File (DMF) matches, returned mail (RPO), or uncashed checks, managing mandatory state due diligence outreach and eventual escheatment remittance to state unclaimed property authorities. |
| **Product** | **Product Eligible Investment Fund** | Product Eligible Investment Fund entity serves as the associative logical rule table linking Products to the specific Investment Funds approved and available for allocation under that product's prospectus or plan menu, along with fund-level allocation caps and minimums. |
| **Product** | **Product Eligible Rider** | Product Eligible Rider entity serves as the associative logical rule table linking base Products to the specific Rider Product Definitions that can be attached at issue or post-issue, capturing state-specific availability and age eligibility overrides. |
| **Experience Studies** | **Experience Study Policy Observation** | Experience Study Policy Observation entity serves as the associative policy-year observation record linking an Experience Study to individual historical Policies, Financial Transactions, and Claims so each policy's exact exposure fraction and decrement outcome can be audited and aggregated into cohorts. |
| **Reinsurance** | **Reinsurance Treaty Participant** | Reinsurance Treaty Participant entity resolves the associative relationship between a Reinsurance Treaty and one or more participating Reinsurer Counterparties (for multi-reinsurer quota share syndicates), recording each reinsurer's share percentage and role. |
| **Party, Customer & Household** | **Household Member Assignment** | Household Member Assignment entity links individual Parties to a Household with effective dates and relationship roles (such as Primary Head of Household, Spouse/Partner, Dependent, or Family Trust), allowing historical tracking of household composition changes. |
